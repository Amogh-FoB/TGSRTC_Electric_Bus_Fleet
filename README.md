# TGSRTC Electric Bus Fleet

This website is a presentation of TGSRTC's electric bus programme and its role in public transport across Telangana. It is intended to give viewers a clear picture of the fleet's scale and its place in the state's transport network.

The presentation brings together the different electric bus categories and the depots where they operate. This helps viewers understand both the vehicles in service and how the electric fleet is distributed.

It can be used to introduce TGSRTC's electric mobility efforts, provide context for discussions about fleet deployment, and share a concise overview with transport stakeholders and the wider public.
